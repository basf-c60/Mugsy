28037: Read a line of input in the answer bubble
Clears the answer bubble on the right of the screen, then reads keys (via 28221) into the buffer at 23808, echoing them in the proportional font. DELETE (12) and LEFT (8) rub out the last character, ENTER (13) ends the line. The line is terminated with a zero byte.
