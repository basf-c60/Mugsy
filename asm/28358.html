28358: VM native: shootout with the hit man from Detroit
Works out Mugsy's hit points from v33 (set by the script from the handout: (low byte of v33 AND 7)+1, or 8 if v33 is 2.56 or more) and plays the shootout at 42112. On return flag 4 (33892) is set to the result: 1 = the hit man was killed, 2 = the hit man was killed but bystanders were hit (the cops then want 20-60% of the safe), 3 = Mugsy was killed.

28360 CALL 31201 BCDE=the handout (as a fixed-point number)
28363 LD A,B Combine the upper three bytes

28366 LD A,E A=the low byte of the handout
28367 JR Z,28371 The upper bytes are zero: use the low byte as it is
28369 LD A,255 The handout is 2.56 or more: use 255 (8 hit points)
28371 CALL 42112 Play the shootout (A gives Mugsy's hit points)
28374 PUSH AF Save the result
28375 LD A,(23611) Read the keyboard flags
28378 RES 5,A Forget any keypress left over from the shootout
28380 LD (23611),A Store the flags
28383 POP AF Restore the result
28384 BIT 0,A Was Mugsy killed (bit 0)?
28386 JR Z,28394 No
28388 LD A,3 A=3: Mugsy was killed
28390 LD (33892),A Store the outcome in flag 4
28393 RET Return
28394 AND 12 Were any bystanders hit (bits 2 and 3)?
28396 LD A,1 A=1: the hit man was killed and no bystanders were hurt
28398 JR Z,28390 Zero: store 1
28400 LD A,2 A=2: the hit man was killed but bystanders were hit
28402 JR 28390 Store the outcome
