29752: Print one decimal digit
Subtracts the power of ten in BC/DE from the number in HL'/HL repeatedly to work out the digit.
29752 PUSH BC Copy the high word of the power of ten into DE'
29753 EXX Switch to the alternate registers...
29754 POP DE ...DE'=high word of the power of ten...
29755 EXX ...and back again
29756 LD C,47 C will count from "0"
29758 INC C Next digit value
29759 AND A Clear the carry flag
29760 SBC HL,DE Subtract the power of ten (low word)...
29762 EXX Switch to the high words...
29763 SBC HL,DE ...and high word
29765 EXX ...and back
29766 JR NC,29758 Repeat until the number goes negative
29768 ADD HL,DE Add the power of ten back on (low word)...
29769 EXX Switch to the high words...
29770 ADC HL,DE ...and high word
29772 EXX ...and back
29773 LD A,(29629) Has a non-zero digit been printed yet?
29776 AND A Test the leading-zero flag
29777 LD A,C A=digit
29778 JP NZ,29509 Print it if so
29781 CP 48 Is it a leading zero?
29783 RET Z Skip it if so
29784 LD (29629),A Otherwise leading zeros are over
29787 JP 29509 Print the digit
