31525: Flood fill
Fills the area containing the pixel at (D,E) solidly with ink, scanning left and right along each row and remembering whether the rows above and below need filling. Every cell it touches also gets A as its ink colour (bits 3-5 of A, if any, are ORed in as paper: the compressed pictures pass 56, meaning ink 0 on paper 7).
Input
A Attribute bits for the filled cells (ink in bits 0-2)
D x coordinate
E y coordinate
