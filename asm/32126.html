32126: Draw a compressed picture
The larger full-screen pictures (the Partridge Club and the street outside Milton's) use a second, more compact format: a 9-byte header (the offset of the end of the point bit-stream, the bit position where it ends, the offset of the attribute data and four bit widths for the coordinate deltas), a bit-stream of points in which each coordinate is stored as a variable-width delta read by 32539, line segments drawn between the points (32612), and finally three run-length encoded passes over the attribute file (ink, then paper, then bright and flash).
