33994: VM native: "A year goes by ......" animation
Installs the IM2 interrupt routine at 34069 (via the jump at 53456 and the vector table at 53504) and then plays one of two short animations chosen at random: the bar-room scene (step table at 36348) or the getaway car (step table at 34298).
Input
Nothing in the game chooses between them: not the year, the hoods, the cash or what happened in the year. The choice is one call of the random routine (30014, limit 100); a result below 50 gives the bar room. That is not an even split, because the routine is not uniform (see 30026): counting the 256 raw values gives 154 for the bar room and 102 for the car, about 60% to 40%.
