42977: Shootout: main loop (the figure dispatcher)
The shootout is a small cooperative scheduler. Each of the six figures (bystander 1, bystander 2, Mugsy, the hit man, Mugsy's bullet and the hit man's bullet) has a timer byte at 42250+n and a handler address in the table at 42238. A timer of 0 means that the figure's countdown is running; the interrupt routine 48539 ticks the countdown and, when it reaches zero, draws the figure's next frame and sets the timer to 1. A timer of 1 tells this routine that the figure is ready for new orders: it runs the handler, which reads the next byte of the figure's route script (or the keyboard, for Mugsy), moves it, and leaves the timer to be decremented back to 0. A timer of 3 parks a figure for good.
