48640: Dictionary
The 225 words that Louey's messages are built from, 1006 bytes in all, in alphabetical order. A message refers to a word by a page number (0-4) and an offset (29928 adds them to 48640), or, for the 32 commonest, by a single byte (the table at 49710). The block starts with a letter index of 32 two-byte entries (64 bytes); the words follow.
Each letter takes one byte: the low five bits are the letter (1 = a to 26 = z; 27 to 31 are the punctuation marks ' - ! ? and ") and the top bits are mostly ignored. Bit 7 marks the last letter of a word, but only from the third letter on, so no word is cut short at two letters. A short word is closed by a byte whose letter is 0. Each line below is one word, with its offset from 48640 (the number a message uses; the page is the offset divided by 256).
The game never reads the letter index: it finds words only through the references. It looks like a by-product of the tool that built the dictionary. 9 words are never referred to by any message: "fin", "fun", "lea", "liv", "min", "per", "pre", "some", "the".
