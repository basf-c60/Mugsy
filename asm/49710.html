49710: Common words
The 32 words that the game uses most, each reachable through a single byte in a message (96-127) instead of the two bytes of an ordinary dictionary reference. 29907 takes the byte minus 96 as an index into this table; each entry is two bytes, the offset of the word in the dictionary at 48640 (which also gives the page), and the page number in the low four bits of the second byte (the routine adds 80 to it to make the word type 5, an ordinary word). The table is in order of use, commonest first, which is why the low token numbers are the busiest. The counts below are the number of times each token appears in the messages.
The commonest are "the" (49), "you" (36), "grand" (22), "da" (19), "ya" (19).
